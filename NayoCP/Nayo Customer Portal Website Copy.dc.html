02 · The problem

Your business runs in IFS. Your partners don't.

So the day-to-day runs on email and phone calls. Where is my order? Has it shipped? Can you resend that invoice? Every question costs your team time, and none of it is self-service.

Giving external partners access sounds simple — until you remember they sit outside your IFS user framework, and each one must see only their own data.

03 · The solution

One portal, built on top of IFS.

Nayo Customer Portal sits on top of your IFS data and opens it up — safely — to the people you do business with. Customers track and place orders. Suppliers work their purchase orders. Your administrators decide exactly who sees what. Nothing is copied or re-keyed: the portal reads and writes the data that's already there.

04 · Key benefits (4 cards)
Real-time IFS data
Orders, shipments, invoices and stock levels come straight from IFS — never an out-of-date export.
No IFS user licences
Portal users operate outside the IFS user framework, so external access doesn't add to your licence count.
Role-based access
Roles and permission sets control what each user can do; customer and supplier mapping controls whose data they see.
Fully customizable
Configure the components, reports and permissions each partner needs, and integrate with the systems around you.
05 · Features — Customer Portal

Self-service for your customers.

  • Orders — see every order and its status, filter and search by any column, and drill from order to line to shipment on one page.
  • Online ordering — place orders directly, with standard IFS part availability checks. Portal orders arrive in IFS as order type WEB, with a status that shows when entry is complete.
  • Shipments & tracking — shipment lines, tracking, weight and freight charges, without a phone call.
  • Invoices — view invoice details and download the invoice document on demand.
  • Part List — each customer's own sales parts and prices, drawn from their IFS agreements and price lists.
  • Part List with Inventory — available quantities by site, so customers check stock before they order.
  • Reports — preview and print IFS reports such as statements of account and price lists.
06 · Features — Supplier Portal

A shared view of every purchase order.

  • Purchase Orders — suppliers see the purchase orders raised for them in IFS, with header details, part lines and no-part lines.
  • Supplier notes — a two-way note on each order, editable in the portal and in IFS, so the conversation stays attached to the order instead of an inbox.
07 · Features — Admin Portal

You decide who sees what.

  • User management — create, edit and remove portal users, and manage credentials and password resets.
  • Roles, groups & permission sets — build roles from permission sets for each application component, and see every user's effective permissions at a glance.
  • Customer & supplier mapping — link each user to their IFS customer or supplier. Coordinators can be mapped to several customers at once.
08 · How it works (3 steps)
  1. Connect — the portal connects to your IFS environment.
  2. Configure — set up roles and permissions, and map users to their IFS customers and suppliers.
  3. Invite — partners log in and see only their own data, live.
09 · FAQ
Do portal users need IFS licences?
No. Portal users operate outside the IFS user framework.
Can a customer see another customer's data?
No. Each user is mapped to their own IFS customer or supplier, and roles control which components they can open.
How do portal orders reach our team?
They're created directly in IFS as order type WEB, with a web order status that changes from "Entry in progress" to "Completed" when the customer finishes.
Can we add our own reports and components?
Yes. The portal is configurable — reports, components and permissions can be tailored to each partner group.
